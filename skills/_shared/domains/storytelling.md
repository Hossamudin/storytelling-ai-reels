# Storytelling & retention — what makes people keep watching

Craft is wasted if the story is flat. Before designing a single scene, answer: **what is the one sentence the viewer
must remember, and why would they care in the first 3 seconds?**

## 1. Understand the voice first (≤ 6 lines, write it in BRIEF.md)

1. The one sentence to remember.
2. The emotional arc in 3 words (e.g. frustration → question → relief).
3. Hook moment and payoff moment (with timestamps).
4. Every concrete noun, number, list, process, contrast and question — with its time.
5. Who is watching and what they already know (from `profile.md`).
6. What would make this "just another video" — so you avoid it.

## 2. Hooks (first 1–3 seconds)

The visual must land before the second word. Patterns that work:
- **Bold claim / number slam**: the most surprising fact, big, with an impact SFX.
- **Pattern interrupt**: an unexpected image or motion that contradicts the topic, then the turn.
- **Question in the viewer's words**: "Still doing X by hand?"
- **Before → after flash**: show the result first, then "here's how".
- **Open loop**: promise a reveal later ("the third one surprised me").
For reels: no logo intro, no "hi guys" before the hook; captions on from frame one.

## 3. Structures

| Video type | Structure |
|---|---|
| Explainer | hook → problem (relatable) → turn (the idea) → 3 proofs/steps → payoff → CTA |
| Tutorial | result preview → what you need → steps (one per section, chapters) → recap → next step |
| Listicle / N tips | hook with the count → items in rising strength (best last or first + last) → recap card → CTA |
| Opinion / myth-bust | myth → why people believe it → evidence → the truth → what to do now |
| Story / case study | situation → struggle → discovery → transformation → lesson |
| News recap | headline stack (what changed) → each story: what / why it matters / one real visual → takeaway |
| Product / tool demo | the pain → the tool in one line → 3 moments it shines (real UI) → price/limits honestly → verdict |

## 4. Rhythm and retention

- A new visual beat every 2–4 s; a hero moment every ~20 s; never static > 4 s.
- Contrast is the engine: before/after, manual/automated, chaos/order, grey/color.
- One visual **spine** (metaphor world) per video, one **signature move** that exists only in this video.
- Re-hook at each section start (a question, a number, a "but…").
- Cut every second that doesn't teach or move the story (retakes, long pauses, silent searching).
- End on the payoff, then the CTA — never after a long goodbye. Outro end screens hold ≥ 5–10 s (YouTube elements).

## 5. CTAs that don't feel cheap

Tie the CTA to the value just delivered: "If this saved you an hour, the next video saves you a day" → end card.
Comment prompts with a keyword ("comment AGENT and I'll send the template") outperform "like and subscribe".
Use the creator's own phrases from the profile (their sign-off, catchphrase).

## 6. Series thinking

- Same brand system, rotating spine families (pipeline → courtroom → lab → map …) so episode 20 ≠ episode 1.
- Keep a log in each project's BRIEF.md: spine, signature move, palette weighting — check it before the next one.
- Reuse library assets (intro sting, lower thirds, end card) for recognition; change the story world.
- A multi-part series from several voice notes: concatenate them (2 s gaps) into one input, split with `parts`, share one
  set of scene builders.

## 7. Platform formats

| Platform | Aspect | Length sweet spot | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok / Reels / Shorts | 9:16 (1080×1920) | 20–60 s | hook in 1 s, captions mandatory, safe zones: keep text out of the bottom ~20% and right edge (UI) |
| YouTube long-form | 16:9 | 6–15 min | chapters, end screen 10–20 s, thumbnail + title designed together |
| LinkedIn | 1:1 or 4:5 | 30–90 s | captions (mostly muted autoplay), professional tone |
| Courses | 16:9 | 3–10 min per lesson | learning aids: steps tracker, recaps, quizzes |
